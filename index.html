5 Recommended Font Combinations

Hand-picked pairings for business portfolios — architectural firms and startups. Each combination shows the actual fonts working together in a hero, tagline, label, and body layout.

🏆 Best for Tech Startups
Modern Startup Standard
Montserrat (Headlines) Inter (Body)
Featured Project

Ship Faster.

A clean, modern pairing adopted by Linear, Vercel, and YC startups.

Inter handles dense body text beautifully, while Montserrat makes every headline pop with geometric confidence.

Recommended for: Tech-forward portfolios, SaaS companies, modern startup websites.
Why it works: Both are geometric sans-serifs that share similar proportions. Inter's neutrality gives Montserrat room to lead the page.
👑 Best for Architects
Editorial Architect
Playfair Display (Headlines) Lora (Body) Montserrat (Labels)
Built Space

Form Follows Function

A timeless serif pairing inspired by museum catalogs and editorial architecture books.

Playfair Display elevates headlines with luxury contrast, while Lora's gentle serifs provide warmth to project descriptions.

Recommended for: Architectural firms wanting traditional authority, editorial portfolios, heritage brands.
Why it works: Two serifs share classical heritage but feel distinct — Playfair leads with drama, Lora whispers with warmth.
🔧 Best for Engineering Firms
Technical Innovator
Space Grotesk (Headlines) Inter (Body) IBM Plex Sans (Labels)
Precision Engineering

Built to Scale

For technical founders and engineers who value clarity over decoration.

Space Grotesk's angular geometry signals technical fluency. Inter grounds the body text. IBM Plex labels add structured authority.

Recommended for: Tech startups, engineering firms, developer-focused portfolios, data-heavy sites.
Why it works: All three fonts share modernist DNA but each has a distinct character — quirky, neutral, and industrial.
💫 Best for Design Studios
Bauhaus Architect Studio
Jost (Headlines & Labels) DM Sans (Body)
Studio Practice

Built from Geometry

Mathematical precision meets geometric elegance — a Bauhaus-inspired identity.

Jost's Bauhaus roots add architectural credibility. DM Sans keeps body text calm and readable. Both share geometric DNA.

Recommended for: Architectural design studios, modern firms wanting mathematical precision, creative practices.
Why it works: Two geometric sans-serifs designed by Bauhaus principles — Jost leads, DM Sans supports.
💎 Best for Premium Brands
Premium Luxury Brand
Bodoni Moda (Headlines) Source Sans 3 (Body) DM Sans (Labels)
Studio Atelier

Refined Details.

Bodoni Moda's thick-thin contrast brings boutique fashion and luxury authority.

Source Sans 3 keeps body text reliable and clear. DM Sans adds crisp metadata. Pair with restraint — only use Bodoni for hero moments.

Recommended for: Luxury brands, fashion houses, premium consulting, high-end interior design portfolios.
Why it works: Bodoni screams. Source Sans whispers — high contrast serif display makes headlines unforgettable, while neutral sans keeps pages usable.
